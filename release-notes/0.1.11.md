## Concrete item attachments

- Clothing attachments now retain and display their concrete item definition,
  such as a mining helmet, instead of the generic `clothingitem` placeholder.
- Construction attachments now retain and display their concrete definition and
  texture variant instead of the generic `constructionitem` placeholder.
- Custom construction colors are preserved across sending, claiming, returning,
  expiry, and recovery.

The Mail database migrates automatically to schema version 3 by adding a
backward-compatible nullable construction-color column. Existing generic
attachments remain readable, but their original concrete definition or color
cannot be reconstructed retroactively.
